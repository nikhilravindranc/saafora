Our Story

Where SAAFORA Began

SAAFORA began its journey in 2005 as SMG Plast Group, serving builders, architects and homeowners with dependable building materials and a reputation for doing things properly.

Over the years, that foundation grew into a trusted name across Kerala and Tamil Nadu. SAAFORA is the sister company of SMG Plast Group, bringing the same values — precision, quality and consistency — to complete interior and exterior solutions, from the building envelope to the finished interior.
